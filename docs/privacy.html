隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日 · English ↓

本地处理

GyroCine 使用相机、麦克风和运动传感器录制视频、声音、陀螺仪、加速度以及相机参数。这些数据在设备上处理，并保存到你选择的 App 存储或外接存储。我们不将录制内容、运动数据或诊断日志上传到开发者服务器，不建立用户账户，不集成广告或行为追踪 SDK。

系统权限

  • 相机：预览和录制视频。
  • 麦克风：同步录制声音。
  • 运动数据：记录运动和重力信息，用于后期增稳及水平辅助。
  • 添加照片：仅在你主动操作时将素材保存到相册。
  • 文件与外接存储：访问你授权的录制目录；App 可保留系统提供的目录授权。

你可在系统设置中管理权限。拒绝必要权限可能使对应功能无法工作。

购买

GyroCine Pro 通过 Apple 的 App 内购买提供。Apple 处理付款和退款；App 在本地验证购买状态。开发者不接收银行卡信息，也不把购买凭证发送到自建服务器。Apple 按其自身隐私政策处理相关信息。

保存、分享与删除

素材、设置和必要的录制诊断保存在本机；你可在素材页删除视频。升级 App 不会主动删除已有素材。卸载 App 可能删除 App 容器及专用存储中的数据，请先导出备份。外接盘、相册、系统备份或云端副本需分别管理。只有在你主动分享或导出时，文件才会交给你选择的目标应用或服务。

支持与网站

本支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 可能处理访问所需的连接信息；主动提交的 Issue 和附件由 GitHub 按其政策处理，公开 Issue 对所有人可见。请勿上传敏感数据。系统崩溃报告和分析共享由你的 Apple 系统设置控制。

联系与更新

隐私问题请通过 项目支持页面 联系开发者。政策变更将公布于此页。

Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

On-device processing

GyroCine uses the camera, microphone and motion sensors to record video, audio, gyroscope, acceleration and camera parameters. Data is processed on-device and stored in the selected app or external storage. We do not upload recordings, motion data or diagnostic logs to developer servers. There are no user accounts, advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking SDKs.

Permissions

Camera access enables preview and recording; microphone access records audio; motion data supports telemetry and horizon guidance. Photos access is used only when you choose to save a clip. File and external-storage access is limited to authorized locations, and system folder authorizations may be retained. Manage permissions in iOS Settings. Denied permissions can prevent the corresponding features from working.

Purchases

Apple handles GyroCine Pro payments and refunds. The app verifies purchase status locally. We do not receive payment-card details or send receipts to our own servers. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Retention, sharing and deletion

Recordings, settings and necessary recording diagnostics are stored locally. Delete videos from Media. App updates do not intentionally delete existing recordings. Uninstalling can remove the app container and dedicated storage; export important files first. External-drive, Photos, backup and cloud copies must be managed separately. Files are sent to another app or service only when you choose to share or export them.

Support and website

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process connection information. Issues and attachments you submit are handled under GitHub policies; public issues are visible to everyone. Do not submit sensitive data. Apple system settings control crash-report and analytics sharing.

Contact and changes

Contact the developer through GitHub Issues. Updates to this policy will be published on this page.
